Drivers and Trends Impact Assessment Mechanism for  Dynamic Technology Roadmap Status Monitoring
Technology roadmaps lose value when external conditions change faster than scheduled review cycles. This project addresses this problem by developing Roadmap Review Application, a prototype for assessing drivers and trends affecting Digital Industry technology roadmaps. Existing Data-Driven Roadmapping research defines roadmap status monitoring, but it gives limited operational detail for turning unstructured textual evidence into a traceable status recommendation. This study fills the gap through a computer science workflow for evidence input, key sentence extraction, PESTLE classification, sentiment direction analysis, impact scoring and roadmap item matching. The system maps evidence into three review labels: Maintain, Adjust and Revise. The prototype supports roadmap analysts by showing severity scores, active PESTLE drivers, evidence traces, matched roadmap items and review advice. Evaluation used ten synthetic Digital Industry scenarios, one roadmap fixture, 104 automated tests and one manual roadmap expert review. All ten scenarios produced the expected status label. A representative Adjust case linked Technological and Legal drivers to four roadmap items. The expert review supported the value of the workflow for review preparation. The main contribution is a traceable evidence-to-status mechanism for roadmap monitoring, while final roadmap judgment remains with experts.
